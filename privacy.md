# Privacy policy

Assistant is a personal script run by one person for themselves only.

- It reads that person's own Gmail and Google Calendar to find school notices and events, and adds events to their own calendar.
- Data from Google is processed on that person's own computer and is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone else, except that short summaries may be processed by an AI model on their behalf to extract dates and actions.
- No one other than the owner uses this app, and no data is collected from anyone else.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
